WBS - Access rights on survey name

This page details the access rights on the questionnaire Test for WBS RFE, and offers to look up whether a particular individual has access to it.

Access rights related to the group Sysadmin Access

Groups/Individuals Vote See (once open) See the results
(specific to that questionnaire) 		Modify (and see before opening date)
Sysadmin Access- includes Systems
Member Access- includes W3C Members, Evangelists, Advisory Board, Technical Architecture Group, Team Access, Invited Experts with Member Access, Chairs, Board of Directors, Others with Member access
W3C Team
Marketing & Communications

Additional rights specific to the questionnaire Test for WBS RFE.
The resulting rights are the union of both rights.
